Prototype Notes

This first working prototype proves the core flow:

Saved activity → recurrence → October dates → AL weekly resident print view.

This demo begins with no activities. Add fictional activities to test schedule conflicts.

New: Monthly Rotation supports different activities on the 1st–5th occurrence of a weekday. Thursday 2:00 and Tuesday Game Show Hour are preloaded as examples.

Also new: Monthly by Week supports activities such as Resident Council (1st Monday), Temple Outing (1st Wednesday), Chair Travel (3rd Tuesday), and Podiatry (4th Tuesday). A one-month move exception can change a date/time without changing the normal recurrence.
